# Two in-page author tutorials

## Goal
Create two polished 16:9 tutorials, each 2–3 minutes long, with voiceover and burned-in captions:

1. **How to use the Marketing Hub**
2. **How to build a funnel**

Each video will appear only on its relevant author page and will start only after the author clicks Play.

## Video 1: How to use the Marketing Hub

Show the real author workflow in this order:

1. Open **Marketing Hub** and select the correct book.
2. Explain the five areas: Overview, Sequences, Social Calendar, Contacts, and Settings.
3. Review campaign readiness and distinguish Build, Review, Activate, and Pause states.
4. Open an email sequence and explain review before activation.
5. Show the copy-paste social workflow: schedule a post, download its graphic, open the chosen social platform, and mark it posted.
6. Show where contacts and campaign settings are managed.
7. Close with the safe workflow: select the book, review the assets, then activate only when ready.

The narration will accurately describe the existing manual social workflow and will not imply automatic social publishing.

## Video 2: How to build a funnel

Show the real author workflow in this order:

1. Open **My Funnels** and select the correct book.
2. Choose a saved product or ABBY suggestion and generate its funnel.
3. Explain the funnel stages and open each stage for review.
4. Edit and save the funnel copy without regenerating approved content.
5. Complete required stage details and explain why Publish remains unavailable until the funnel is ready.
6. Publish the funnel, copy its public link, and show the live result.
7. Briefly show pause and book-association controls without creating duplicate funnels or assigning unattributed funnels automatically.

## Visual and audio direction

- Use the Authors Bureau navy, gold, teal, and indigo visual system.
- Use clean screen-led motion, cursor emphasis, restrained zooms, and short title cards rather than generic stock footage.
- Record against a safe demonstration account/book with no sensitive personal details visible.
- Use one calm, professional voice across both videos.
- Burn captions into the picture and keep all important controls readable at 16:9.
- End both videos with **Authors Bureau — Your Story is Your LIFE**.

## Page placement

- Add a shared **TutorialVideo** presentation that shows a poster image, title, duration, and Play control.
- Place the Marketing Hub tutorial directly below the page introduction and above book selection and tabs.
- Place the funnel tutorial directly below the My Funnels introduction and above book selection and funnel statistics.
- Open the player inline or in a focused overlay after Play; do not autoplay.
- Include standard playback controls, captions, sound, fullscreen, and a close action.
- Keep the page fully usable if the video cannot load.

## Production and verification

1. Capture the current workflows from the live author experience so the tutorials match what authors actually see.
2. Write and time both scripts before recording.
3. Produce the two MP4 files, voiceovers, captions, and poster frames.
4. Inspect representative frames and verify narration, caption timing, readable controls, and no exposed private information.
5. Add the finished media and player to the two pages only.
6. Verify desktop and mobile layouts, click-to-play behavior, sound, captions, fullscreen, and playback failure handling.
7. Test both tutorials on the live site after an isolated publication containing only these tutorial additions.

## Technical scope

Expected app areas:

- Marketing Hub page presentation
- My Funnels page presentation
- One shared tutorial player component
- Two MP4 assets, two caption tracks, and two poster images
- Focused playback and placement tests

No campaign, funnel, book, pricing, payment, publication, or author-content records will be changed.
