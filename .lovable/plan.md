# Homepage Conversion and Professionalism Audit

## Goal
Make the public website feel credible, polished, easy to scan, and focused on turning published authors into qualified sign-ups without exposing prices.

## Work plan

1. **First-screen clarity**
   - Make the central promise immediately understandable.
   - Use the locked strategic message: “Your book is not the business. Your book is the HOOK.”
   - Remove unsupported “#1” positioning and excessive promotional language.
   - Keep one dominant action and one lower-emphasis learning path.

2. **Trust and proof**
   - Replace unsupported income statistics and absolute automation claims with specific, defensible outcomes.
   - Tighten founder credentials and author stories so they read as proof rather than marketing filler.
   - Keep claims tied to visible platform capabilities and real author/book examples.

3. **Visual hierarchy and consistency**
   - Standardise author portraits to the documented head-and-shoulders rule.
   - Standardise book-cover proportions, text lengths, and card heights.
   - Reduce oversized headings, decorative pills, excessive italics, and competing gold accents.
   - Keep the established navy, gold, and teal identity while improving contrast and restraint.

4. **Page flow and copy**
   - Remove repeated explanations and consolidate overlapping sections.
   - Reorder the page into a logical decision path: promise → proof → process → examples → author results → action.
   - Shorten paragraphs for scanning and use benefit-led headings.
   - Remove em dashes and inconsistent terminology.

5. **Conversion paths**
   - Make calls to action consistent and accurately describe the next step.
   - Verify sign-up, sign-in, directory, featured-author, founder, and “How It Works” links.
   - Preserve hidden public pricing and the $100 personal-selling rule.

6. **Quality verification**
   - Review the finished page at desktop and mobile widths.
   - Check image loading, cropping, font sizing, colour contrast, text wrapping, spacing, and interactive states.
   - Run source checks and a live browser link pass before reporting completion.

## Technical notes
- Changes stay within public-facing presentation and copy unless a broken link requires a routing correction.
- Existing design tokens and shared controls remain authoritative.
- The audit will use established usability principles: concise scannable copy, clear purpose, visible proof, restrained presentation, and outcome-led calls to action.
