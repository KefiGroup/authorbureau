---

## 1. What it is

Curated bundles of two or more existing offers sold as a single discounted unit. Cross-sell engine.

## 2. What ABBY builds

Engine: **Commerce**. ABBY suggests profitable bundle combinations and writes the sales-page copy.

## 3. What the author does

1) Open BA-17 builder. 2) Pick ≥ 2 items from existing live offers. 3) Set bundle price. 4) Activate.

## 4. What the reader experiences

Reader sees bundle on microsite OR at checkout upsell → buys all items together.

## 5. Readiness gate (`hasRequiredAssets`)

`title` set AND ≥ 2 items in `items[]` AND commerce signal. (A 'bundle' of one is just the underlying product.)

_Source: `supabase/functions/_shared/node-readiness.ts`._

## 6. Revenue model

Direct — increases AOV by 30–60 % per `mem://business/node-revenue-architecture-estimates`.

## 7. Dependencies

≥ 2 live commerce nodes (BP-06/07/09, BA-10/12, etc.).
