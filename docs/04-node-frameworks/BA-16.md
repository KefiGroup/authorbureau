# BA-16 · Affiliates

_Version 3.2 · 2026-05-01_

**Category:** Build Authority  
**Scope:** Author-level  
**Primary edge function:** `supabase/functions/generate-ba16-affiliate/index.ts`

> Canonical source: see [Master Architecture Reference](../01-architecture/01-master-architecture-reference.md) §3 for the full registry.

---

## 1. What it is

Affiliate programme letting other creators promote the author's products for a referral cut.

## 2. What ABBY builds

Engines: **CRM + Commerce**. ABBY drafts affiliate sales page, terms, recruitment email, tracked links. NODE_NAME resolves dynamically via `getCanonicalNodeLabel('BA-16')` → 'Affiliates' (Sprint 51 replaced legacy 'Affiliate Programme').

## 3. What the author does

1) Open BA-16 builder. 2) Set commission %. 3) Approve recruitment kit. 4) Activate. 5) Recruit affiliates manually.

## 4. What the reader experiences

Reader follows an affiliate link → standard checkout → affiliate attribution recorded.

## 5. Readiness gate (`hasRequiredAssets`)

Generic gate — non-empty `content_json` passes.

_Source: `supabase/functions/_shared/node-readiness.ts`._

## 6. Revenue model

Indirect (each affiliate sale shares revenue).

## 7. Dependencies

Stripe; one or more paid offers (BP-06/07, BA-10/12, etc.) to promote.
