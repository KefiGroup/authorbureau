# BA-16 · Affiliates

_Version 3.0 · 2026-05-01_

**Category:** Build  
**Scope:** Author-level  
**Edge function:** `supabase/functions/generate-ba16-affiliate/index.ts`

---

## 1. What it is

An affiliate programme letting other creators earn a commission for promoting the author's offers.

## 2. What ABBY builds

Engines: **CRM + Commerce**. ABBY drafts affiliate-recruitment copy, terms, tracking links.

## 3. What the author does

1) Open BA-16 builder. 2) Set commission rate. 3) Approve terms. 4) Activate; share affiliate-signup URL.

## 4. What the reader experiences

Affiliate signs up → gets unique referral link → reader purchases → commission attributed.

## 5. Readiness gate (`hasRequiredAssets`)

Generic gate.

_Source: `supabase/functions/_shared/node-readiness.ts`._

## 6. Revenue model

Negative direct (commission paid out) but expands top-of-funnel reach.

## 7. Dependencies

Stripe (for affiliate payout via Connect).
