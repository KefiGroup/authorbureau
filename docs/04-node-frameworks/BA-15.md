# BA-15 · Media & PR

_Version 3.0 · 2026-05-01_

**Category:** Build  
**Scope:** Author-level  
**Edge function:** `supabase/functions/generate-ba15-media-pr/index.ts`

---

## 1. What it is

Press kit, press release, target media outlets list — the assets needed to pitch the book to journalists, podcasters, and influencers.

## 2. What ABBY builds

Engine: **Asset store**. ABBY generates press release (headline + body), pitch email template, target outlets list (`target_media_outlets`).

## 3. What the author does

1) Open BA-15 builder. 2) Review press release. 3) Review outlets list. 4) Activate. (Pitching itself is manual.)

## 4. What the reader experiences

(B2B — journalist receives pitch.)

## 5. Readiness gate (`hasRequiredAssets`)

Press release present (string OR object with headline AND body) AND outlets list non-empty.

_Source: `supabase/functions/_shared/node-readiness.ts`._

## 6. Revenue model

Indirect — earned media drives book sales + authority.

## 7. Dependencies

None.
