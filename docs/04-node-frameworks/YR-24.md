---

## 1. What it is

In-person multi-day retreats — premium experience tied to the book's transformation.

## 2. What ABBY builds

Engines: **Sessions + Commerce**. ABBY drafts retreat itinerary, sales page, application form.

## 3. What the author does

1) Open YR-24 builder. 2) Set venue + dates. 3) Set price. 4) Activate.

## 4. What the reader experiences

Reader applies → accepted → buys → travels to venue.

## 5. Readiness gate (`hasRequiredAssets`)

`title` set AND commerce signal AND (`session_type` OR `booking_url` — session-style).

_Source: `supabase/functions/_shared/node-readiness.ts`._

## 6. Revenue model

Direct — typically $3k–$15k per seat.

## 7. Dependencies

Stripe; venue logistics manual.
