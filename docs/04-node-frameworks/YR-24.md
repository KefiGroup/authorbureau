# YR-24 · Retreats

_Version 3.0 · 2026-05-01_

**Category:** Yield  
**Scope:** Author-level  
**Edge function:** `supabase/functions/generate-yr24-retreats/index.ts`

---

## 1. What it is

Multi-day in-person retreats led by the author at a chosen location.

## 2. What ABBY builds

Engines: **Sessions + Commerce**. ABBY drafts retreat agenda, sales page, application form.

## 3. What the author does

1) Open YR-24 builder. 2) Set dates, location, capacity, price. 3) Activate.

## 4. What the reader experiences

Reader applies → pays → travels → attends retreat.

## 5. Readiness gate (`hasRequiredAssets`)

`title` + commerce signal + session-style fields.

_Source: `supabase/functions/_shared/node-readiness.ts`._

## 6. Revenue model

Direct — typically $3k–$15k per attendee.

## 7. Dependencies

Stripe; logistics handled out-of-band.
