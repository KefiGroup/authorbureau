# YR-26 · Conference

_Version 3.0 · 2026-05-01_

**Category:** Yield  
**Scope:** Author-level  
**Edge function:** `supabase/functions/generate-yr26-conference/index.ts`

---

## 1. What it is

An annual conference branded around the author's framework — multi-speaker, multi-day, sponsor-supported.

## 2. What ABBY builds

Engines: **Commerce + Sessions**. ABBY drafts conference theme, agenda template, sponsor deck (cross-pushes to YR-28).

## 3. What the author does

1) Open YR-26 builder. 2) Set dates + venue. 3) Define ticket tiers. 4) Activate.

## 4. What the reader experiences

Reader buys ticket → attends conference.

## 5. Readiness gate (`hasRequiredAssets`)

`title` + commerce signal.

_Source: `supabase/functions/_shared/node-readiness.ts`._

## 6. Revenue model

Direct — multiple ticket tiers + sponsorship revenue.

## 7. Dependencies

Stripe; logistics out-of-band.
