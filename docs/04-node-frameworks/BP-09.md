# BP-09 · Book Sales

_Version 3.0 · 2026-05-01_

**Category:** Brand  
**Scope:** Book-level  
**Edge function:** `supabase/functions/generate-bp09-speaking/index.ts`

---

## 1. What it is

The canonical sales channels for the book itself — Amazon link, sales page, optional MoR direct sale.

## 2. What ABBY builds

Engine: **Commerce**. ABBY generates sales page copy (mandatory 11-section framework, see `mem://features/standardized-sales-page-builder`), exports slides + handout via `export-bp09-slides` / `export-bp09-handout`.

## 3. What the author does

1) Open BP-09 builder. 2) Paste Amazon / retailer URLs. 3) Optionally enable direct platform sale. 4) Activate.

## 4. What the reader experiences

Reader visits microsite → clicks Buy → routed to Amazon OR completes platform checkout.

## 5. Readiness gate (`hasRequiredAssets`)

`title` set AND (`amazon_url` OR `sales_page_url` OR commerce signal).

_Source: `supabase/functions/_shared/node-readiness.ts`._

## 6. Revenue model

Direct — book sale margin (Amazon royalty or 92% of MoR price).

## 7. Dependencies

BP-04 (microsite to host the link).
