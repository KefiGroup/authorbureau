# BP-09 · Book Sales

_Version 3.2 · 2026-05-01_

**Category:** Brand Products  
**Scope:** Book-level  
**Edge function:** `supabase/functions/generate-bp09-book-sales/index.ts`

> Canonical source: see [Master Architecture Reference](../01-architecture/01-master-architecture-reference.md) §3 for the full registry.

---

## 1. What it is

The book itself as a paid product — Amazon link, sales page on the microsite, and conversion toolkit (decks, scripts) for live audiences.

## 2. What ABBY builds

Engine: **Commerce**. ABBY generates sales-page copy plus a Book Sales kit — live-audience pitches, decks, and scripts for workshops, signings, and corporate lunches.

## 3. What the author does

1) Open BP-09 builder. 2) Paste Amazon link OR enable platform checkout. 3) Approve sales-page copy. 4) Activate.

## 4. What the reader experiences

Reader clicks → either Amazon (external) OR `<BuyNowButton>` → confirmation + download / shipping.

## 5. Readiness gate (`hasRequiredAssets`)

`title` set AND (`amazon_url` set OR `sales_page_url` set OR commerce signal).

_Source: `supabase/functions/_shared/node-readiness.ts`._

## 6. Revenue model

Direct — typically $9.99–$24.99 per unit.

## 7. Dependencies

BP-04 microsite for sales page.
