# BP-02 · Lead Magnet

_Version 3.0 · 2026-05-01_

**Category:** Brand  
**Scope:** Book-level  
**Edge function:** `supabase/functions/generate-bp02-lead-magnets/index.ts`

---

## 1. What it is

A short (2–3 minute) assessment, quiz, or downloadable that captures a reader's email in exchange for value tied directly to the book's promise.

## 2. What ABBY builds

Engine: **Funnel Engine**. ABBY generates an 8-question quiz with 5 result tiers, 3 headline variants, a 4-stage conversion microsite (Gate → Quiz → Results → Next Step), and a Social Distribution Pack. Stored in `funnels` and `marketing_assets`.

## 3. What the author does

1) Open BP-02 builder. 2) Pick a template (6 visual templates) and tone. 3) Review the 8 generated questions; edit if needed. 4) Pick one of 3 headline variants (Identity / Outcome / Curiosity). 5) Click Activate — the lead-magnet microsite goes live at a public URL.

## 4. What the reader experiences

Reader sees the gate page → submits email → takes the 8-question quiz → lands on a personalised result tier → is invited to a next step (book purchase, BP-01 enrolment, or another offer).

## 5. Readiness gate (`hasRequiredAssets`)

Generic gate — any non-empty `content_json` passes. The lead-magnet builder always saves substantial structured content on activation.

_Source: `supabase/functions/_shared/node-readiness.ts`._

## 6. Revenue model

Indirect — primary feeder for BP-01 and downstream paid offers.

## 7. Dependencies

BP-01 (Email Engine must exist for enrolment). Optionally pushes asset packs to BP-03 and BP-04.
