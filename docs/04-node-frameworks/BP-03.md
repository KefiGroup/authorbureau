## 1. What it is

A copy-paste content factory (Sprint 61). ABBY generates 20–30 platform-tuned posts with graphics and drops them in the Marketing Hub Social Calendar. The author taps **Copy caption** + **Download graphic** + **Open LinkedIn / Facebook / Instagram**, pastes, publishes, and marks as posted (~20 sec per post). **No auto-posting** — honest manual workflow. Step 4 of the builder is labelled **"Send to Social Calendar"**, not "Activate". The `hasAnyConnection` gate was removed.

## 2. What ABBY builds

Engines: **content store** + Social Designer. ABBY generates the calendar with `generate-social-content`. The 2-step `compose-social-post` (background → burn-in) renders graphics across 6 templates × 5 platforms with brand-kit auto-pull.

## 3. What the author does

1) Open BP-03 builder. 2) Generate the 30-day calendar. 3) Use Design All to render every post graphic. 4) Activate. 5) Copy + paste posts manually to each platform.

## 4. What the reader experiences

Reader sees the post on a social platform → clicks through to lead magnet OR microsite → enters the funnel.

## 5. Readiness gate (`hasRequiredAssets`)

Calendar generated (`posts_generated > 0` OR `content_calendar_id` set OR `posts[]` non-empty). No external connector check.

_Source: `supabase/functions/_shared/node-readiness.ts`._

## 6. Revenue model

Indirect — top-of-funnel discovery.

## 7. Dependencies

None (manual posting). Optional cross-push: BP-02 lead magnet for CTA.

## Technical Appendix

### 8. `content_json` schema

```typescript
{
  content_calendar_id?: string;
  posts_generated: number;
  posts: Array<{
    id; platform: "instagram"|"linkedin"|"twitter"|"facebook"|"tiktok";
    caption; image_url?; scheduled_at?: string; status: "draft"|"designed"|"posted";
  }>;
}
```

> Stored at `author_nodes.content_json` for this node ID. The readiness gate (§5) reads from this object.
