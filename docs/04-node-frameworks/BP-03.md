# BP-03 · Social Media

_Version 3.0 · 2026-05-01_

**Category:** Brand  
**Scope:** Author-level  
**Edge function:** `supabase/functions/generate-bp03-social-media/index.ts`

---

## 1. What it is

A 30-day calendar of social posts in the author's voice, plus an on-demand graphic composer (Social Designer) that produces platform-sized images.

## 2. What ABBY builds

Engines: **Funnel + content store**. ABBY generates a calendar of platform-tagged posts and stores them in `social_media_content` / `social_posts`. The Social Designer (`compose-social-post`) produces 6 templates × 5 platforms via 2-step background + burn-in image rendering.

## 3. What the author does

1) Generate or refresh the 30-day calendar. 2) For any post, click Design to render a graphic. 3) Copy text + download image. 4) Post manually to chosen platforms (Buffer was removed; manual control is intentional).

## 4. What the reader experiences

Reader sees the post on the platform → clicks through to the lead magnet, microsite, or Buy link → enters the funnel.

## 5. Readiness gate (`hasRequiredAssets`)

`posts_generated > 0` OR `content_calendar_id` set OR `posts[]` non-empty.

_Source: `supabase/functions/_shared/node-readiness.ts`._

## 6. Revenue model

Indirect — top-of-funnel awareness driving traffic to lead magnets and sales pages.

## 7. Dependencies

None required. Recommended: BP-02 lead magnet so social CTAs have a destination.
