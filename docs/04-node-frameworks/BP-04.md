# BP-04 · Author Website

_Version 3.1 · 2026-05-01_

**Category:** Brand Products  
**Scope:** Book-level  
**Edge function:** `supabase/functions/generate-bp04-website/index.ts`

> Canonical source: see [Master Architecture Reference](../01-architecture/01-master-architecture-reference.md) §3 for the full registry.

---

## 1. What it is

The book's public microsite — hero, about, lead-magnet form, learn page, and revenue offers — hosted on Authors Bureau.

## 2. What ABBY builds

Engine: **Microsite renderer** + content stores. ABBY generates hero copy, headline, subheadline, about (long + short), and section content. Public URL pattern: `authorsbureau.com/<author-slug>/<book-slug>`.

## 3. What the author does

1) Open BP-04 builder. 2) Review hero + about + sections. 3) Add a lead magnet (BP-02 cross-push auto-fills). 4) Activate. Owner-preview lets the logged-in author preview unpublished drafts.

## 4. What the reader experiences

Reader arrives via search / social / referral → reads hero + about → submits email to lead magnet → enters BP-01 sequence → returns to browse Learn / Buy sections.

## 5. Readiness gate (`hasRequiredAssets`)

Primary anchor (`hero_headline` non-empty OR `sections[]` non-empty) AND ≥ 1 supporting field (`about_long`, `about_short`, `hero_subheadline`, OR `lead_magnet_id`).

_Source: `supabase/functions/_shared/node-readiness.ts`._

## 6. Revenue model

Indirect — hub for every other node. Direct revenue when sections render `<BuyNowButton>` for BP-06/07/09 or YR offers.

## 7. Dependencies

Recommended: BP-02 (lead magnet), BP-09 (book sales). Microsite copy must respect `mem://content/public-site-logic` (no pricing visible, no em-dashes, hides empty fields).
