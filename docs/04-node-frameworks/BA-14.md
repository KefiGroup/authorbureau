# BA-14 · Podcast Tour

_Version 3.0 · 2026-05-01_

**Category:** Build  
**Scope:** Author-level  
**Edge function:** `supabase/functions/generate-ba14-podcast/index.ts`

---

## 1. What it is

The author's own podcast — ABBY drafts seasons, episode outlines, show notes, and pushes to Transistor.fm for RSS distribution.

## 2. What ABBY builds

Engine: **Podcast** (Transistor.fm). `generate-podcast-season` produces season arcs; `deploy-ba14-to-transistor` syncs episodes.

## 3. What the author does

1) Open BA-14 builder. 2) Generate season. 3) Record episodes. 4) Deploy to Transistor → distributed to Apple/Spotify automatically.

## 4. What the reader experiences

Listener subscribes on Apple / Spotify → episodes auto-deliver via RSS.

## 5. Readiness gate (`hasRequiredAssets`)

RSS ready (`rss_url`/`rss_feed_url`/`transistor.show_id`) AND ≥ 1 episode, OR activated + ≥ 2 episodes + show title (strict isLive check per `mem://audits/manus-2026-04-23-corrections`).

_Source: `supabase/functions/_shared/node-readiness.ts`._

## 6. Revenue model

Indirect — authority + audience. Sponsorship revenue routes via YR-28.

## 7. Dependencies

Transistor.fm (platform-managed).
