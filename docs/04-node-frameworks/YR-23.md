## 1. What it is

An ongoing high-ticket peer group of vetted members meeting on a recurring cadence.

## 2. What ABBY builds

Engines: **Sessions + Commerce**. ABBY drafts mastermind charter, member criteria, monthly format, application form.

## 3. What the author does

1) Open YR-23 builder. 2) Define cadence + price. 3) Activate.

## 4. What the reader experiences

Applicant applies → vetted → pays → joins monthly sessions + community.

## 5. Readiness gate (`hasRequiredAssets`)

`title` + commerce signal + session-style fields.

_Source: `supabase/functions/_shared/node-readiness.ts`._

## 6. Revenue model

Direct + recurring — typically $1,000–$5,000 / month.
