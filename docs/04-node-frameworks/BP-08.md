# BP-08 · Special Editions

_Version 3.0 · 2026-05-01_

**Category:** Brand  
**Scope:** Book-level  
**Edge function:** `supabase/functions/generate-bp08-mastermind/index.ts`

---

## 1. What it is

Limited-edition bundles tied to the book — signed copies, bonus content, anniversary editions.

## 2. What ABBY builds

Engine: **Commerce**. ABBY generates marketing copy + bonus content descriptions; bundles stored in `special_editions` + `special_edition_bundles`.

## 3. What the author does

1) Open BP-08 builder. 2) Define bundle contents + bonus assets. 3) Set price + inventory. 4) Activate.

## 4. What the reader experiences

Reader sees the bundle on microsite → buys → receives confirmation + (if physical) shipping detail collection.

## 5. Readiness gate (`hasRequiredAssets`)

Generic gate.

_Source: `supabase/functions/_shared/node-readiness.ts`._

## 6. Revenue model

Direct — premium pricing ($49–$199).

## 7. Dependencies

BP-09 (book sales) and BP-04 (microsite).
