## 1. What it is

Sponsorship packages for the author's podcast (BA-14), conference (YR-26), and content channels.

## 2. What ABBY builds

Engine: **Commerce**. ABBY drafts sponsor deck, package tiers, audience metrics summary.

## 3. What the author does

1) Open YR-28 builder. 2) Define sponsor packages + price. 3) Activate.

## 4. What the reader experiences

Sponsor (B2B) reviews deck → contracts → receives placement.

## 5. Readiness gate (`hasRequiredAssets`)

`title` + commerce signal.

_Source: `supabase/functions/_shared/node-readiness.ts`._

## 6. Revenue model

Direct — $1k–$50k per sponsor per period.

## 7. Dependencies

BA-14 (podcast) and/or YR-26 (conference) for inventory.
