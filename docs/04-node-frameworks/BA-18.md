## 1. What it is

Joint-venture deals with other authors — co-promote each other's offers to combined lists.

## 2. What ABBY builds

Engine: **CRM**. ABBY drafts JV pitch emails, partner brief, swap terms.

## 3. What the author does

1) Open BA-18 builder. 2) Identify candidate partners. 3) Send pitch via Email Engine. 4) Track in CRM.

## 4. What the reader experiences

Reader on partner's list receives co-promo → enters author's funnel.

Generic gate.

## 6. Revenue model

Indirect (audience swap) + revenue-share splits on JV launches.
