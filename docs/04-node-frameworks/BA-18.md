## 1. What it is

Cross-promotion deals with other authors — list-swaps, joint webinars, shared launches.

## 2. What ABBY builds

Engine: **CRM**. ABBY drafts pitch templates, partnership scope docs, swap agreements.

## 3. What the author does

1) Open BA-18 builder. 2) Approve pitch templates. 3) Activate. 4) Outreach manually to peer authors.

## 4. What the reader experiences

Reader joins via partner author's list → enters BP-01 sequence.

## 5. Readiness gate (`hasRequiredAssets`)

Generic gate — non-empty `content_json` passes.

_Source: `supabase/functions/_shared/node-readiness.ts`._

## 6. Revenue model

Indirect — list growth + cross-promotion.

## 7. Dependencies

BP-01 (sequence to enrol new contacts into).
