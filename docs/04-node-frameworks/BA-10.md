# BA-10 · Online Course

_Version 3.0 · 2026-05-01_

**Category:** Build  
**Scope:** Book-level  
**Edge function:** `supabase/functions/generate-ba10-online-course/index.ts`

---

## 1. What it is

A premium, video-based course expanding the book into a full curriculum. Hosted on Thinkific, sold by Authors Bureau as MoR.

## 2. What ABBY builds

Engines: **Course + Commerce**. ABBY drafts module/lesson curriculum (16k token complex generation), validates against sales copy via Mismatch Validator (`mem://features/sales-curriculum-validation-system`). Deploys via `deploy-ba10-to-thinkific`.

## 3. What the author does

1) Open BA-10 builder. 2) Review modules. 3) Approve sales page (auto-validated against curriculum). 4) Connect Thinkific. 5) Activate.

## 4. What the reader experiences

Reader buys → enrolled in Thinkific automatically → progresses through modules → certificate on completion (optional).

## 5. Readiness gate (`hasRequiredAssets`)

`title` set AND (`course_id` OR ≥ 1 module OR commerce signal).

_Source: `supabase/functions/_shared/node-readiness.ts`._

## 6. Revenue model

Direct — typically $297–$997.

## 7. Dependencies

Thinkific connector. Sequenced after BP-07 per Product Development Sequence.
