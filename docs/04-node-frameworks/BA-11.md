# BA-11 · Audiobook

_Version 3.0 · 2026-05-01_

**Category:** Build  
**Scope:** Book-level  
**Edge function:** `supabase/functions/generate-ba11-audiobook/index.ts`

---

## 1. What it is

An audiobook version of the book — TTS-narrated with ElevenLabs, packaged for ACX, distributed manually.

## 2. What ABBY builds

Engine: **TTS pipeline** (ElevenLabs V2). ABBY generates narration script, voice preview (`ba11-voice-preview`), and the ACX submission guide. Save & Distribute flow handles packaging.

## 3. What the author does

1) Open BA-11 Audiobook Studio. 2) Pick voice. 3) Generate narration script. 4) Review chapters. 5) Generate audio (`elevenlabs-tts-audiobook-v2`). 6) Download ACX-ready package; submit to ACX manually.

## 4. What the reader experiences

Listener buys on Audible / Apple Books / etc — Authors Bureau is the producer of record on the ACX submission.

## 5. Readiness gate (`hasRequiredAssets`)

`narration_script_url` set OR `acx_guide_generated === true` OR `chapters[]` non-empty.

_Source: `supabase/functions/_shared/node-readiness.ts`._

## 6. Revenue model

Direct — Audible royalty (paid through ACX, not Authors Bureau Commerce).

## 7. Dependencies

ElevenLabs (platform-managed). ACX account is author's responsibility.
